Data: GGG currency exchange (Faustus) for hourly trades, and poe.ninja for daily history, Uniques and Divination Cards. Refreshed hourly by a GitHub Actions job. Not affiliated with Grinding Gear Games or poe.ninja. Source: github.com/Strix-YT/poe-market-trends
How the numbers work

Hourly (1h, 6h, 24h): for currency-exchange items, each hour's price is the volume-weighted average rate on Faustus for the last completed hour (GGG publishes each hour once it ends, so the newest point is up to an hour old). Uniques and cards aren't traded on Faustus, so their hourly moves come from hourly poe.ninja snapshots. Where hourly history is too short, 24h falls back to poe.ninja's daily change.

7-day forecast: prices are converted to Divines, then a damped-momentum model projects the latest daily moves forward (75% of the last day's change plus 25% of the day before, shrinking 45% a day, capped at roughly ±80% a day). Gain or Lose means a projected move beyond ±10% in Divines. Confidence follows liquidity: currency by Chaos traded per day (High ≥ 20,000c, Medium ≥ 3,000c), Uniques by trade listings (High ≥ 100, Medium ≥ 25).

This is a short-term momentum read, not trading advice. Patches, new strategies and meta shifts move prices in ways no trend line can see.
