How the forecast works, and its limits

Data: poe.ninja's Currency Exchange for the PoE 1 Covens league: Currency, Fragments, Essences, Fossils, Resonators, Oils, Delirium Orbs, Omens, Tattoos, Artifacts, Divination Cards, Runegrafts, Allflame Embers and Astrolabes. A GitHub Actions job pulls fresh prices every hour. Items trading under 50 chaos a day are left out because their prices are mostly noise. Scarabs have no exchange data in this league yet. Each item's daily price is converted to Divines first, because chaos itself is falling fast against Divine.

Forecast: a damped-momentum model. Each item's expected daily move is 75% of its last day's change plus 25% of the day before (in log terms). That move then shrinks by 45% each day over the next seven. "Gain" means more than +10% in Divines over 7 days, "Lose" means more than −10%, and anything between is "Hold". Confidence follows daily traded volume: High is 20,000c or more, Medium 3,000c or more, Low is below that. Items with only two days of trading are capped at Medium.

  • poe.ninja's price history is daily, so an hourly refresh mainly updates today's price. The forecast is a short-term momentum read, not a model of game mechanics. A patch, a farming-strategy discovery or a crafting meta shift can flip any of these in hours.
  • League-start prices are noisy. Catalysts, Oils, Lifeforce and basic orbs usually keep sliding as supply floods in. Crafting items tied to new mechanics usually rise until the meta settles.
