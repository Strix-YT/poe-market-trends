    How the numbers work

    Hourly (1h, 6h, 24h): for currency-exchange items, each hour's price is the volume-weighted average rate on Faustus for the last completed hour (GGG publishes each hour once it ends, so the newest point is up to an hour old). Uniques and cards aren't traded on Faustus, so their hourly moves come from hourly poe.ninja snapshots. Where hourly history is too short, 24h falls back to poe.ninja's daily change.

    Two 7-day predictions, both measured against the Divine Orb. 7d trend assumes the current momentum continues: 75% of the last day's change plus 25% of the day before, fading 45% a day. 7d past leagues is what the same item did over the next 7 days at this point in past leagues (median across leagues), or its category when the item has too little history (marked c). Call (Gain / Hold / Lose) blends the two in the way that came true most often when replaying the last five leagues day by day (—): it expects big recent moves to partly pull back and adds half the category's usual move. Gain or Lose means a projected move beyond ±10%. Confidence follows liquidity: currency by Chaos traded per day (High ≥ 20,000c, Medium ≥ 3,000c), Uniques by trade listings (High ≥ 100, Medium ≥ 25).

    This is a short-term momentum read, not trading advice. Patches, new strategies and meta shifts move prices in ways no trend line can see.
