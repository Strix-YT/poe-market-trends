How the numbers work

Hourly prices for currency, fragments, essences and cards come from GGG's currency exchange (Faustus): the volume-weighted rate in the last completed hour. Uniques come from hourly poe.ninja snapshots. The 7-day outlook converts prices to Divines and projects the latest daily momentum forward, fading it each day. "Rising" and "Falling" outlooks mean a projected move of more than 10% against the Divine Orb. Liquidity is how much trades per day (currency) or how many are listed (uniques). This is a short-term trend read, not trading advice.
